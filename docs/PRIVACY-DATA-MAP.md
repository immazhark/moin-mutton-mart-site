## Release and incident checklist

- Review plain-language privacy notice, contact channel, responsibility and request handling with owner and qualified advisor under applicable Indian privacy obligations. No blanket DPDP compliance claim. Official framework: https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2190655&lang=1&reg=3 ; confirm applicable dates and actual obligations at release. Confirm owner contact for rights/complaints, identity verification proportional to request, correction/deletion/withdrawal workflow and retention exceptions. Do not request extra identity data by default.
- Inventory actual Cloudflare/Firebase/WhatsApp provider settings, logs, transfers, subprocessors and retention before publication; no unverified deletion guarantee. Review lawful processing, privacy notice at collection/handoff, customer communications and breach response. No tracker/cookie banner added for nonexistent marketing tracking; revisit consent if services change. Terms/refunds and accessibility notice require factual owner approval; no fictional policies.
- On suspected owner account compromise: revoke active membership through trusted console, secure Google account, preserve relevant audit evidence, review suspicious revisions, assess affected data and applicable notice duties with advisor. Do not erase audit history, blindly restore a backup or promise deletion of external records. Re-enable access only after verified recovery. Actual owner/admin role and incident response process require acceptance.
- Browser storage is not confidential against same-origin scripts or device access. OWASP guidance: https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/HTML5_Security_Cheat_Sheet.html and https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/Session_Management_Cheat_Sheet.html. Limit recovery to validated catalogue operation in tab scope, no extra credentials or PII, and keep CSP deployment/verification gate. Explicit same-operation retry after fresh membership; no automatic writes. GOV.UK error summary/check answers patterns informed clear status and draft review: https://design-system.service.gov.uk/components/error-summary/ and https://design-system.service.gov.uk/patterns/check-answers/.
