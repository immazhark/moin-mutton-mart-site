# Approved design direction — 25 November 2026

User approved the B + C hybrid: Modern Market storefront with Signature Gold branding.

- Compact charcoal header, existing gold logo, restrained gold type/details.
- White product cards on cool pale gray; maroon category selection; green ordering controls.
- Actual weight in steppers, never ambiguous packet counts. Optional per-item preparation notes and separate order note.
- No full-screen hero. Catalogue starts in first mobile viewport.
- All rates and availability need shop confirmation until a live backend is verified.
- Owner editor will have a shop-wide display-prices / ask-rate switch retaining saved prices, plus product overrides and separate availability. This extends the original catalogue contract and must be covered in security rules and audit snapshots before live integration.
- Chicken pricing/weight basis remains unconfirmed. Proposed quantities are request preferences, not guaranteed sold portions.
- Original logo retained. Neutral photo placeholders explicitly labelled until approved real photographs arrive.
## Permanent design system — 28 November 2026

Read [binding master directive](directives/MASTER-DESIGN-ENGINEERING-DIRECTIVE.md) and [project interpretation](directives/BINDING-INTERPRETATION.md). The approved B+C identity above remains the baseline; the new design programme is an iterative enhancement, not a new theme. One big idea: Fresh, clear, one tap away. Compact real-photo-led signature moment only after genuine assets and accurate hours are approved; maintain immediate catalogue access. Mobile typography, semantic tokens, concentric radii, balanced headings, tabular money/quantities, optical alignment, clear interaction states and restrained CSS-only motion must earn their cost. Keep the current system fallback until a measured subset improves craft without budget regression. Document component states, responsive behavior, accessibility, reduced motion and actual render evidence here as refinements are approved. Target 44x44 controls, 8px spacing, 60 KB gzip customer JS and 500 KB first-view. No decorative clutter, heavy libraries, fake imagery or claims. Research and technique decisions are tracked in [living matrix](qa/DESIGN-IMPLEMENTATION.md); the approved nine-phase risk-first plan governs design work. Do not invent visual scores; render, critique, refine, optimize and verify.
