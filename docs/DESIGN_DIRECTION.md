# Approved design direction — 25 November 2026

User approved the B + C hybrid: Modern Market storefront with Signature Gold branding.

- Compact charcoal header, existing gold logo, restrained gold type/details.
- White product cards on cool pale gray; maroon category selection; green ordering controls.
- Actual weight in steppers, never ambiguous packet counts. Optional per-item preparation notes and separate order note.
- No full-screen hero. Catalogue starts in first mobile viewport.
- All rates and availability need shop confirmation until a live backend is verified.
- Owner editor will have a shop-wide display-prices / ask-rate switch retaining saved prices, plus product overrides and separate availability. This extends the original catalogue contract and must be covered in security rules and audit snapshots before live integration.
- Chicken pricing/weight basis remains unconfirmed. Proposed quantities are request preferences, not guaranteed sold portions.
- Original logo retained. Neutral photo placeholders explicitly labelled until approved real photographs arrive.
## Permanent design system — 28 November 2026

Read [binding master directive](directives/MASTER-DESIGN-ENGINEERING-DIRECTIVE.md) and [project interpretation](directives/BINDING-INTERPRETATION.md). The approved B+C identity above remains the baseline; the new design programme is an iterative enhancement, not a new theme. One big idea: Fresh, clear, one tap away. Compact real-photo-led signature moment only after genuine assets and accurate hours are approved; maintain immediate catalogue access. Mobile typography, semantic tokens, concentric radii, balanced headings, tabular money/quantities, optical alignment, clear interaction states and restrained CSS-only motion must earn their cost. Keep the current system fallback until a measured subset improves craft without budget regression. Document component states, responsive behavior, accessibility, reduced motion and actual render evidence here as refinements are approved. Target 44x44 controls, 8px spacing, 60 KB gzip customer JS and 500 KB first-view. No decorative clutter, heavy libraries, fake imagery or claims. Research and technique decisions are tracked in [living matrix](qa/DESIGN-IMPLEMENTATION.md); the approved nine-phase risk-first plan governs design work. Do not invent visual scores; render, critique, refine, optimize and verify.

## Preliminary design and platform research (28 November 2026; not a rendered design pass)

- [Impeccable source skill](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable/blob/main/skill/SKILL.src.md): reviewed its creative-direction and audit/critique guidance. Use it to examine brand specificity, hierarchy and state quality in Phase 8, not as a runtime library. No dependency installed; compare against the existing approved B+C identity and verify actual render. Full catalogue of required visual references remains due before significant visual work.
- [Baymard cart quantity research](https://baymard.com/research-articles/auto-update-users-quantity-changes): public search excerpt supports direct plus/minus quantity controls; full article retrieval was unavailable. Retain unit-aware steppers, visible remove controls and clear quantity/stock boundaries. No claim of a full UX study.
- [GOV.UK check answers](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/patterns/check-answers/) and [validation recovery](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/patterns/validation/): use editable summaries, precise error recovery, and preserved inputs for the WhatsApp review and owner drafts. Do not copy government visual identity.
- [WCAG 2.2 target size](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-minimum) and [focus not obscured](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/focus-not-obscured-minimum): test actual hit rectangles, focus and sticky-bar behavior; our 44x44 CSS px/8px requirement remains stricter.
- [web.dev code splitting](https://web.dev/learn/performance/code-split-javascript) and [JavaScript evaluation/long tasks](https://web.dev/articles/script-evaluation-and-long-tasks): verify configured cold-load waterfall, parse cost and route isolation; lazy loading alone does not guarantee 60 KB first-load compliance.
- [MDN env()](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/Reference/Values/env), [text-wrap](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/Reference/Properties/text-wrap), [corner-shape](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/Reference/Properties/corner-shape) and [view transitions](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/View_Transition_API/Using): native safe-area handling and balanced headings are appropriate; use ordinary radius fallback and test feature detection; any view transition must be optional and must not delay navigation or defeat reduced motion. No experimental dependency for decorative corners.
- [Provenance Village Butcher delivery information](https://provenancebutcher.com/pages/delivery-more-info): real retail reference for distinguishing weight from piece count and explaining delivery conditions. Apply only the principle; never copy another shop's promises, facts or images.

Design research is a staged input, not permission to introduce animation libraries or change the visual direction before security/accessibility/performance gates. The current source-only anti-slop audit remains AUDIT-0; use fresh before/after renders and both directive rubrics during the authorized visual phase. Review additional industry/award/reference sites then; no invented scores.
